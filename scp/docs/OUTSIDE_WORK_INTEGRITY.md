# Outside-work and material-reversal integrity: 18 November 2026

The initial enterprise controls merged through [PR #40](https://github.com/Hasen1506/Claude-versioned/pull/40), main `4ff0d5f02f4d72e84e0790268d98da897ebcdd7d`, after all three remote CI jobs passed. This continuation traces outsourced production and direct shipping through physical custody, supplier billing, WIP and reversal accounting. It extends the earlier audit; it does not certify every manufacturing exception or SAP equivalence.

## Raw evidence and independent fixture

The before probes ran on the clean merged main above. The same standalone reproduction script runs against the repaired source with fresh in-memory stores and synthetic identities. Its JSON records source identity, dirty-tree state, HTTP result, revision and literal ledger balances. No production data or external messages are used.

The small reference fixture starts with 100 components at 4 each, zero finished goods and a finished standard price of 10. A job worker receives 20 components to make ten finished units, with a service charge of 2 per finished unit. Receiving nine finished units, taking two unused components back, billing and paying the service charge must leave 82 components plus nine finished units: stock 418, bank −18, and zero WIP, AP, suspense and price difference. This is independent arithmetic, not a number copied from the implementation or a SAP reference tenant.

## Findings and repairs

| Confirmed finding | Failure mechanism and consequence | Smallest defensible repair and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Posted outside-work references could be deleted or rewritten — high | On clean main, deleting a billed challan was accepted as revision 2: WIP changed from zero to −20, AP remained −20, and suspense became +20. Deleting a billed direct-ship order also introduced suspense +20. The journal classified charges from current object membership, while the shared save boundary did not retain those objects. Physical custody quantities and recorded consumption were also replaceable. | `companies/outside.py` retains posted challans, sent/billed direct-ship orders, their original commercial/ownership terms and existing custody/dispatch events. New custody effects must match new material movements; cumulative consumption/returns/losses cannot exceed sent parts. Cancellation requires all original parts back. Whole/patch/merge attacks, normal staged operations and literal WIP/AP balances are exercised in `test_jobwork_integrity.py` and `test_direct_ship_integrity.py`. |
| Reversal identity and cumulative quantity were not enforced on raw saves — high | A five-unit scrap movement could receive a 500-unit reversal through an ordinary company save. In the fixture, stock value rose from 380 to 2,380 with a spurious −2,000 price difference. A balanced journal and self-derived stock reconciliation did not establish a legitimate business event. Membership was still required; this is an integrity failure within authorized write paths. | `companies/reversals.py` requires an original non-reversal, causal posting order, the same product/place/lot/type/ownership, compatible serials and a cumulative quantity no greater than the original. The shared material guard applies it to all persistence paths. Challan-held issues can only return through the matching custody operation. `test_reversal_integrity.py` covers whole/patch/merge attacks, changed identity, duplicate/excess quantity, missing originals and legitimate cumulative partial reversals. |
| Material reversal lost its original balancing contra account — high | The journal retained explicit counter-lines but omitted the balancing WIP/COGS/scrap/count account added by `_post`. Its reversal therefore used price differences. The independent nine-unit fixture left WIP +8 and price difference −8 after unused components came back and the service bill was paid. | `accounting/__init__.py`, `_Journal._movement`, captures the actual original non-stock/non-valuation-difference voucher lines. Stock and valuation differences continue to come from the stock books. Reversals now use the original contra account, including its entity and party. The fixture clears WIP and price difference to zero; a scrap/reversal test clears the original expense and retains the earlier dated report. |
| Later custody events rewrote earlier dated custody views — high/medium | An explicit view on the dispatch date initially showed 20 components held by the worker; after a future processed receipt, the same view showed none. Future cancellations also removed earlier period dispatch rows. Custody operations could explicitly predate dispatch. | Explicit dated custody views now filter future dispatches/returns and apply cancellation on its recorded closing date. Period dispatch rows retain challans cancelled after the period. Custody events cannot precede dispatch or the previous recorded event. An omitted legacy action date falls back to at least the most recent custody event. Tests compare earlier holdings and dispatch-period rows before/after later events. |

The source checks are enforced, not merely documented. Typed challan/direct-ship models are validated before lenient draft parsing can discard a malformed posted object. Malformed custody collections are rejected as validation/authorization failures without an internal error or revision change. New objects cannot reuse an already posted unknown reference to change its financial classification. A loss at the worker additionally needs the `inventory_adjustment` grant; sending the direct-ship supplier order needs `purchasing` authority when the named matrix is configured. Empty matrices retain the previously documented legacy behavior.

## Authoritative execution

The existing company command contract now accepts `kind: outside`, using the typed outside-action adapter. Dispatch, processed receipt, unused return and cancellation read authoritative state and share the command's explicit date, revision check, authorization, atomic persistence/audit and retry receipt. A dispatch replay after later receipt and billing returns current state without sending or receiving again. It does not imply that every legacy browser action has migrated from preview/save to commands.

The built-image acceptance script now traverses the independent outsourced-production fixture through HTTP commands, checks the exact stock/WIP/AP/bank/suspense/price-difference amounts, retries the original dispatch and refuses deletion of the posted challan. Existing purchasing/action/save checks remain in the same smoke test.

## Verification and limits

The published CI configuration also runs the PostgreSQL cases. All **five selected browser workflows** passed: job work, direct shipping, accounts and the two stock/count journeys. The generated API schema, web build, Ruff and diff checks passed. These suites overlap; their counts must not be added as unique cases. The actual-image smoke has been extended with the independent fixture above; its published-head CI result must be checked before merge.

Source and dependency identities, the before/after reproduction, raw logs and hashes accompany the downloadable evidence. CI validates the published head before merge; a green state is not assumed from previous local runs.

The corrected journal changes previously incorrect **derived account classifications** for existing material reversals. It retains source movements and invoices. Before replacing approved external books, reconcile affected periods, review the WIP/COGS/scrap/count and price-difference deltas, and obtain controller acceptance. Unknown legacy custody, reversal, cancellation or external-bank facts are not manufactured.

The dated-view repair covers custody events and cancellation cutoffs. The same response's planning-worklist section still uses the current scenario. Complete statutory acceptance and snapshots of jurisdiction-specific party/product tax master data have not been established. The generated ITC-04 data has not been certified against an actual configured reference system.

Full decimal/currency support, separate document and accounting dates, standard-cost revaluation, complete manufacturing labor/WIP/variance closure, remaining bin/lot/wave/quality and correction exceptions, representative planning concurrency/quality, real interface certification, HA and measured RPO/RTO remain acceptance/engineering work.
