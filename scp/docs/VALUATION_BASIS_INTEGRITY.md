## Independent acceptance

The small standard-cost fixture has 10 finished units at 70 and receives 100 components at standard 8 against a purchase price of 10. Expected stock is **1,500**, GR/IR **−1,000**, opening equity **−700**, and price difference **200**. Switching the valuation method must return 403 without changing the revision or these balances. Changing future duty/freight/handling must preserve the earlier report. The corresponding moving-average fixture is independently checked for historical immutability.

A new ten-unit purchase of a second component at price 5 and standard 4, after handling rises to 2, must use that new line's handling basis while retaining the original line's zero handling. Expected aggregate stock is **1,540**, GR/IR **−1,050**, opening equity **−700**, delivery **−20**, and price difference **230**. The records must show zero handling on the earlier line and 2 on the new line.

Checks include whole saves, patches and merges; closed and open periods; invoices before goods; effective-method equivalence; changed product dimensions; forged/omitted/duplicate/malformed valuation data; setup on-hand, planning epoch and resource-cost changes; and omitted/rewritten openings. Property-based tests vary duty and handling for both supported valuation methods and compare the complete earlier journal. Existing ERP/Shopify/Tally tests distinguish their external events by business reference and explicitly check retained setup quantities; duplicate imports must leave the entire movement list unchanged.

The actual-image HTTP acceptance script also changes duty and handling after the independently reconciled paid 1,000 receipt. The entire previous accounting report must remain unchanged; attempts to forge the stored base or switch the posted valuation method return 403 with no revision change. Its result belongs to the CI job for the published source, not to a previous image or host-only fixture. Final source identities and actual results accompany the downloadable evidence.

## Compatibility and limits

The first authorized ERP stock count can establish an opening instead of an unposted setup estimate when the company has no previous financial activity. Other setup stock is retained. This preserves the existing initialization workflow; it is not permission to replace a posted opening. Initial company imports still need independently approved opening data. A legacy snapshot preserves the basis known in the previously saved company; it cannot recover earlier true prices or quantities already overwritten before the repair.

Formerly virtual setup vouchers acquire stable `OP-` identities and become retained material rows on the next applicable persistence operation. Actual imported or posted material document identifiers remain unchanged. Integration consumers must identify external events by `erp_ref`/document identity rather than treating every row as a new ERP event. Reconcile previously exported opening voucher identities before re-exporting approved external books; no production migration or external-book replacement was performed during this investigation.

The retained terms are server-derived metadata, not a new business grant or a bypass for financial changes. Named departmental grants and existing place/family/membership limits still apply to the requested operation. Closed-period checks continue to govern new dated events. Retaining an already reported opening is a normalization of its known facts, not an invented inventory adjustment or recovery of unknown migration history.
